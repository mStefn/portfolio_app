Who am I

I work mainly with Linux, Docker, AWS and CI/CD, with a focus on DevOps and infrastructure. Most of what I know comes from building and maintaining my own environments and putting things together myself rather than only learning the theory.

I’ve worked with Docker, GitHub Actions, Ansible, networking, monitoring and AWS services, and I’m currently developing my skills in Kubernetes and Terraform. I like working on infrastructure, automating things that don’t need to be done manually and figuring out why something isn’t working when it breaks.

This portfolio is a collection of some of the projects I’ve built along the way. I’m still adding to it as I work on new projects and learn new tools.

Outside of tech, I’m into progressive metal, running and the gym. I also travel to concerts quite often, including shows outside Poland. And when I just want to switch off, I usually play some games.
