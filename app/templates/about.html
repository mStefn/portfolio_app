Who am I

My main focus is Linux and infrastructure. Most of my practical experience comes from building and maintaining my own systems rather than only learning the theory.

I work with Linux servers, Docker, networking, AWS, CI/CD and monitoring. A big part of my learning comes from solving problems when something breaks — whether it is a deployment issue, a networking problem, DNS configuration or a server running out of resources.

I prefer learning by building things and figuring out how they work. My projects started as simple experiments, but over time I have added automation, CI/CD, monitoring, security and ways of dealing with real infrastructure problems. I am currently developing my skills in Kubernetes, Terraform and Ansible.

Before getting into IT, I spent many years working with CNC machines and technical programming. That experience taught me to work independently, troubleshoot problems under pressure and pay attention to details — things that I find equally useful when working with infrastructure.

Outside of tech, I spend quite a lot of time around music and sport. I am a big fan of progressive and extreme metal, collect vinyl records and travel to concerts whenever I get the chance. I also run and spend plenty of time at the gym.

And when I am not doing any of that, there is a good chance I am playing something.
